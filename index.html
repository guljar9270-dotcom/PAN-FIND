Step-by-Step Guide

How to Find Lost PAN Number by Aadhaar

Retrieve your forgotten Permanent Account Number (PAN) quickly through our agent portal or official government portals.

01

Login or Visit Official Portal

Sign in to PANFind.site for instant assisted search, or visit incometax.gov.in under "Instant e-PAN".

02

Enter 12-Digit Aadhaar

Provide your valid 12-digit Aadhaar number carefully to initiate the PAN Number Find lookup.

03

Instant Verification

Verify via OTP on official government portals, or process instantly through your agent dashboard on PANFind.site.

04

Retrieve & Download e-PAN

Get your 10-digit alphanumeric PAN number immediately and download e-PAN PDF via Protean (NSDL) or UTIITSL.

Why You Might Need to Pan Find

  • Lost Physical Card: Retrieve your PAN number to apply for duplicate PAN reprint.
  • Income Tax Filing (ITR): Mandatory requirement for filing annual tax returns.
  • Banking & KYC: Needed for opening bank accounts, demat accounts, and loans.
  • Aadhaar-PAN Linking: Keep your PAN active and compliant with Income Tax rules.

Official Government & Authorized Portals

  • Income Tax Portal: incometax.gov.in (Instant e-PAN & Link Status)
  • Protean (NSDL PAN): onlineservices.nsdl.com (New PAN & Reprint)
  • UTIITSL PAN: pan.utiitsl.com (Authorized PAN Services)
  • Parivahan Sewa: parivahan.gov.in (Official RC & DL Portal)
Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PAN Find, digital verification tools, and platform usage.

How can I find my lost PAN number using Aadhaar?

If your mobile number is linked with Aadhaar, you can check your PAN details and download Instant e-PAN through the official Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in). PANFind.site also offers fast assisted PAN Find search and B2B APIs for registered agents and businesses.

What services are available inside the Agent Dashboard?

Registered users can access PAN Find, NSDL New/Correction/Minor PAN Apply, RC & DL PDF servers, DL Number Find, Voter Mobile Link & PDF, AgriStack Farmer Card, Ration Card Find, Birth/Domicile/Gazette Certificates, ABHA Card, and GST/IFSC/Pincode utilities.

How do I get API access for my own website?

Click on Register to create your account, then log in to your dashboard to view API documentation, request/response samples, and generate your API key.
