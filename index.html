Complete Guide: How to Pan Number Find by Aadhaar

Learn how to retrieve your lost or forgotten Permanent Account Number (PAN) via our assisted portal or official government websites.

01

Access Portal or Official Site

Login to PANFind.site for instant agent search, or visit the official Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in) under "Instant e-PAN".

02

Enter 12-Digit Aadhaar

Input your valid 12-digit Aadhaar number carefully to initiate the Pan Number Find verification process.

03

Verify Request

On the official portal, verify via 6-digit Aadhaar OTP. On our agent dashboard, process securely from your account.

04

Retrieve & Download e-PAN

Get your 10-digit alphanumeric PAN number immediately and download e-PAN PDF or order reprint via NSDL / UTIITSL.

Why You Might Need to Pan Find

  • Lost Physical Card: Need PAN number to apply for duplicate PAN card reprint.
  • Tax Filing (ITR): Urgent requirement of PAN for Income Tax Return submission.
  • Banking & KYC: Opening a bank account, demat account, or high-value transactions.
  • Aadhaar-PAN Linking: Unlinked PAN cards can become inoperative under the Income Tax Act.

Official Government & Authorized Portals

You can also access official PAN & Transport services directly through government portals:

  • Income Tax e-Filing: incometax.gov.in
  • Protean (NSDL PAN): onlineservices.nsdl.com
  • UTIITSL PAN: pan.utiitsl.com
  • Parivahan Sewa: parivahan.gov.in

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common questions about PAN Find, RC/DL PDF, Voter services, and legal compliance.

How can I find my lost PAN number using Aadhaar?

If your mobile number is linked with Aadhaar, you can check your PAN details and download Instant e-PAN through the official Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in). PANFind.site also provides fast assisted search tools and B2B APIs for agents and businesses.

What services are included in the Agent Dashboard?

Registered agents get access to PAN Find, NSDL New/Changes/Minor PAN Apply, RC & DL PDF servers, DL Number Find, Voter Mobile Link & PDF, AgriStack Farmer Card, Ration Card Find, Birth/Domicile/Gazette Certificates, ABHA Card, and GST/IFSC/Pincode utilities.
